Our Story

A Legacy of Flavor

Founded in 1995 in the heart of Mumbai, Gazala Restaurant began as a small family kitchen with a singular dream — to share the rich, authentic flavors of Indian cuisine with the world.

For three generations, we've preserved our grandmother's secret recipes, blending them with modern culinary artistry. Every dish carries the warmth of our heritage and the care of our family.

Today, Gazala stands as one of the city's most loved dining destinations — where guests become family and meals become memories.
